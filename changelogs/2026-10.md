# Changelog 2026-10

- Cross-view navigation
  – “Show on PCB” now available from schematic viewer & synchronised focus across schematic⇄PCB⇄3D (schematic-viewer #283, pcb-viewer #1068, runframe #5568, 3d-viewer #1015).

- Real-time part availability & cost
  – JLC price/stock shown in schematic part details (schematic-viewer #282).
  – New parts-engine backend with JLC lookup (parts-engine #61); availability warnings enabled in core & CLI by default (core #4424/#4429, cli #5180).

- Return-current simulation workflow
  – New Circuit-JSON schema (#887) and SVG/Canvas rendering of selected results (circuit-to-svg #818, circuit-to-canvas #304).

- Schematic placement QA
  – Detector rules for resistors across pins, mis-aligned RC branches, backwards LED chains, etc. (schematic-placement-analysis #211/#218/#215).

- Visual & fabrication fidelity
  – Filled fabrication-note paths preserved everywhere (circuit-to-svg #812, circuit-to-canvas #300, easyeda-converter #595, cli #5176).
  – Dark tented-via centres rendered in SVG/Canvas/GLB (core #4432, circuit-to-svg #814, circuit-to-canvas #304, circuit-json-to-gltf #242).
  – Pill-pad solder-paste generation added (core #4434).
  – PCB viewer now remembers chosen rendering engine (pcb-viewer #1072).

- Assembly & modelling
  – Generic assembly.part and reference-surface support (core #4444, props #919, circuit-json #891).
  – Footprinter allows custom pin-row courtyards (#908).
  – Extensive mechanical library expansion: T-slot profiles, shaft collars/couplers, springs, bearings, hardware, hollow arm tube, etc. (modelprinter #20-38, jscad-electronics #409-445) with GLB downloads & camera presets on svg.tscircuit.com (#2476/#2484).
  – Explicit adapter & motor cable models with automatic pin-1 orientation (jscad-electronics #424/#426, cableprinter #10).

- CLI & tooling
  – CLI pops availability warnings by default, updates EasyEDA converter, drops old svg-xray alias (#5180, #5176, #5201).
  – TI CLI surfaces actionable CC2340 pin errors (#257) and handles bidirectional capability (#259).

- Reliability fixes
  – Copper-pour false-intersection bug squashed (checks #396).
  – Core warns on non-default schematic sheet sizes & keeps fixed-size sheets at origin (#4440/#4456).
