# Changelog 2026-10

- Cross-view navigation
  – Show on PCB added to schematic viewer ([tscircuit/schematic-viewer #283](https://github.com/tscircuit/schematic-viewer/pull/283)) and wired through runframe (runframe #5568) with a new PCB-side focus controller (pcb-viewer #1068).

- Real-time part availability & pricing
  – JLC cost/stock shown in schematic part details (schematic-viewer #282).
  – New parts-engine JLC lookup (parts-engine #61) surfaced as opt-in warnings in core (core #4424, #4429) and enabled by default in CLI (cli #5180).

- Richer schematic QA
  – Updated placement analysis 0.0.46 (schematic-viewer #284, cli #5188) plus new detectors for misplaced resistors, LED chains, RC branches (c-json-schematic-placement-analysis #211, #215, #218).
  – Sheet-style warning schema & render (c-json #881, circuit-to-svg #815) with non-default sheet size alerts (core #4440).

- Improved fabrication graphics
  – Filled fabrication-note paths preserved across converters & renderers (easyeda-converter #595, circuit-to-svg #812, canvas #300, webgpu #20).
  – Dark tented-via centers rendered in SVG/Canvas/3D (core #4432, circuit-to-svg #814, canvas #304, gltf #242).

- Assembly & mechanical modeling overhaul
  – Generic assembly.part support end-to-end (props #919, core #4444).
  – Explicit adapter cables and pin-1-aligned connectors (cableprinter #10, c-json #878, core #4420, jscad-electronics #424).
  – 30+ new mechanical model contracts & renderers (modelprinter #20-#43, jscad-electronics #406-#445).

- 3D & viewer enhancements
  – GLB downloads + camera presets in svg.tscircuit.com (svg.tscircuit.com #2476, #2484).
  – 3D viewer now shows cad_cable, reorders context menu, and bumps canvas version (3d-viewer #1015-#1017).
  – PCB viewer remembers rendering-engine preference (pcb-viewer #1072).

- Simulation & analysis
  – Return-current simulation data visualised in SVG (circuit-to-svg #818) with supporting schemas (c-json #887).

- Export robustness
  – STEP exporter merges coplanar faces & keeps holes (jscad-to-step #17).
  – Parasolid pipeline fixes face ordering and native entity support (jscad-to-parasolid #1-#3, parasolidts #1-#3).

- Miscellaneous
  – Footprinter gains custom courtyard sizing (footprinter #908).
  – Checks eliminate false copper-pour shorts on tiny edges (checks #396).
  – CLI cleans up obsolete aliases (cli #5201).
