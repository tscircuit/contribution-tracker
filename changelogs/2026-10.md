- Cross-view navigation: “Show on PCB” in schematic (#283) with runframe support (#5568) and PCB viewer focus controller (#1068).
- Part availability & pricing: JLC price/stock shown in schematic (#282); CLI now checks availability by default (#5180); parts-engine adds JLC lookup (#61); opt-in supplier warnings in core (#4424).

- Assembly features: generic assembly.part objects in core/props (#4444, #919) and new assembly reference-surface schema (#891, #923).

- Better visuals: dark centers on tented vias across SVG/Canvas/GLB (#4432, #814, #304, #242) and filled fabrication note paths rendered everywhere (#812, #300, #20).
- Schematic QA: placement analysis now flags resistors across pins, mis-aligned RC branches and backward LED chains (#211, #218, #215) – shipped with viewer/CLI 0.0.46.
- Manufacturing accuracy: solder-paste for pill pads (#4434), custom pin-row courtyard dims (#908) and preserved bus target lengths (#4446).
- Datasheet discipline: auto-datasheet enrichment limited to chips/op-amps (#4428).
- Key fixes & UX polish: persist PCB renderer preference (#1072); keep fixed-size sheets at origin (#4456); remove duplicate silkscreen contours (#4419); false copper-pour intersection fix in checks (#396).
