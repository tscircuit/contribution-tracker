# Changelog 2026-10

- Cross-view navigation – “Show on PCB” in the schematic viewer with matching focus in Runframe / PCB-viewer (#283, #5568, #1068)
- Supply-chain insight – live JLC price & stock, new parts-engine lookup and CLI availability checks on by default (#282, parts-engine #61, #5180)
- Fabrication graphics – filled fab-note paths & uniform opacity everywhere (SVG, Canvas, EasyEDA, PCB-viewer, GLB) plus dark tented-via centers (#812, #300, #595, #1074, #4432, #814, #304, #242)
- Assembly & 3D – generic assembly.part support + color/material props (#4444, #919) and >25 new mechanical model contracts / renderers (T-slot, shaft collars, bearings, springs, grommet, fasteners, etc.) in modelprinter & jscad-electronics (#20-38, #405-445); cad_cable now shows in 3D viewer (#1017)
- Smarter cables – automatic plug alignment from computed pin-1 positions & explicit pin-side orientation (#4420, #424); generic adapter cable composition (#cableprinter-10)
- PCB quality – solder-paste for pill pads (#4434), custom courtyard overrides (#908), preserved bus target length (#4446), and fixed tiny copper-pour DRC false-positives (#396)
- Schematic guidance – new placement checks for resistors across pins, mis-aligned RC branches, backward LED chains (#211, #218, #215) plus sheet-size/style warnings (#4440, #815)
- Simulation overlays – return-current simulation schema & SVG viewer rendering (#887, #818); continued AM3352 SBC routing & crosstalk tooling (#42-43)
- Export improvements – STEP / Parasolid exports merge co-planar faces & keep holes (#17, jscad-to-parasolid #1-3); multiple Altium / EasyEDA converter fixes
- Misc – persist PCB render-engine preference (#1072) and remove obsolete CLI alias (#5201)
