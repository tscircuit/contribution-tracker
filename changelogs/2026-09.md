# Changelog 2026-09

- Autorouter usability: added a "simplify" preset [#723](https://github.com/tscircuit/props/pull/723) and new simplification phase [#3581](https://github.com/tscircuit/core/pull/3581)
- Autorouter quality: Pipeline-11 trace simplification [#2335](https://github.com/tscircuit/tscircuit-autorouter/pull/2335) and same-net copper handoff acceptance [#2361](https://github.com/tscircuit/tscircuit-autorouter/pull/2361)
- Routing fixes: removed duplicated traces [#3583](https://github.com/tscircuit/core/pull/3583) and preserved fan-out handoff identities [#3603](https://github.com/tscircuit/core/pull/3603)
- Plated-pill pad clearance corrected [#257](https://github.com/tscircuit/checks/pull/257)
- Copper pours now clear around oval plated holes [#86](https://github.com/tscircuit/copper-pour-solver/pull/86)
- Schematic-trace solver: better ground-label placement, dog-leg removal, rail merging & crossing prevention (e.g. [#1054](https://github.com/tscircuit/schematic-trace-solver/pull/1054), [#1052](https://github.com/tscircuit/schematic-trace-solver/pull/1052), [#1017](https://github.com/tscircuit/schematic-trace-solver/pull/1017))
- SVG service can render PCB debug objects on demand [#2186](https://github.com/tscircuit/svg.tscircuit.com/pull/2186)
- Image downloads now export every schematic sheet [#4729](https://github.com/tscircuit/tscircuit.com/pull/4729)
- Editor UX fixes: file-URL sync [#4748](https://github.com/tscircuit/tscircuit.com/pull/4748) and lockfile auto-open disabled [#4734](https://github.com/tscircuit/tscircuit.com/pull/4734)
- CLI: prevented race conditions during concurrent "tsci add" installs [#4590](https://github.com/tscircuit/cli/pull/4590)
- KiCad export: added support for KiCad v10 property flags [#67](https://github.com/tscircuit/kicadts/pull/67)
- Altium parsing: full Bus/BusEntry support [#119](https://github.com/tscircuit/altiumts/pull/119) and preservation of antenna copper & polygon cut-outs [#105](https://github.com/tscircuit/circuit-json-to-altium/pull/105)
- Gerber export: skipped zero-length via transitions to avoid stray copper [#154](https://github.com/tscircuit/circuit-json-to-gerber/pull/154)
- TI library: added MSPM33C321x/M0 MCUs and sub-circuit picker with category filtering [#224](https://github.com/tscircuit/ti/pull/224), [#225](https://github.com/tscircuit/ti/pull/225), [#227](https://github.com/tscircuit/ti/pull/227)
