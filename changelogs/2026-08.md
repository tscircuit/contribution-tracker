# Changelog 2026-08

- Differential–pair DRC: require strict point-to-point routing (rfc#13, core#3011) and clearer ambiguity warnings (#3017)
- Autorouter evolution: named phase events + preserved pre-routed traces (#3020-21), Pipeline9 support (core#3023, #3057, props#788) and stitched-trace / relaxed-DRC fixes (autorouter#1927, #1950-54)
- PCB pack solver: new timeout setting (core#3015) with CLI flag fixes for placement DRC (#4071)
- Schematic solver/layout: cross-sheet net-label handling (#3036), automatic decoupling & rail-load alignment upgrades (core#2962, matchpack#202-04)
- 3D viewer overhaul: physically-based materials, realistic relief, grid & lighting tweaks; trace textures clip at pours (3d-viewer#960, #964-#968)
- Viewers/Rendering: SVG & Canvas clip/group traces at copper-pours and add silkscreen graphics (svg#654, #658; canvas#258); PCB-viewer and 3D clip pours too (pcb-viewer#932, 3d-viewer#965)
- Footprinting/Manufacturing: default circular BGA pads (#760), asymmetric QFN & linear SMD support (#770, #781), rectangular pad rotation preserved in Gerber (#134)
- Data/Diagnostics: schematic traces carry source_trace_id (circuit-json#688) and new diagnostic categories (util#113); CLI gains source checks, rats-nest preview & autorouter-phase debugging (#4052, #4058, #4062)
- Performance: faster packing via cached mappings & indexed lookups (calc-packing#119-21); exporters refactored for speed & reliable board outlines (gltf#176, step#125)
- Libraries & Examples: dozens of new TI power/Bluetooth subcircuits and complete Bluetooth speaker system design (ti#100-109, system-block-designer#65)
